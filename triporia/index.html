A semester abroad, a consultation with a specialist in another country, a move to a new city: each brings its own schools, clinics, insurers and agents. We liaise with them on your behalf and keep you informed at the moments that matter.

Services

  • Travel.

    Itineraries, transport and accommodation built around your plans, for leisure or business.

  • Education.

    Help for students and families considering study abroad, from shortlisting options to applications and the practicalities of arrival.

  • Healthcare.

    Appointments with medical providers abroad, arranged together with travel and accommodation for the patient and anyone accompanying them.

  • Insurance.

    Finding and arranging suitable cover for travel, health, property or relocation.

  • Real estate.

    Buying, renting or relocating abroad: shortlists, viewings and liaison with local providers.
