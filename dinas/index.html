Why clients come to us

Ask most growing companies how many advisers they use and the answer is “too many”. The accountant hasn’t seen the marketing plan; the lawyer hears about the new pricing after launch. Each piece of advice is sound on its own – it’s the joins between them that cost money. We were set up to work on those joins.

How an engagement runs

It starts with a conversation about what you’re trying to achieve and what stands in the way. We then look at the parts of the business that bear on that question and tell you plainly where the problems are. If there is work to do, you get a written scope – what we recommend, who will do it and when – and we stay involved while your team puts it in place.
