Building materials, timber, metal structures and associated equipment, for projects large and small.

1.Scope of supply

  • 1.1Building materials:

    cement, aggregates, blocks, insulation and finishing materials.

  • 1.2Timber:

    sawn timber, plywood, OSB and formwork panels.

  • 1.3Metal products:

    reinforcing steel, structural sections, profiles and fixings.

  • 1.4Equipment:

    scaffolding, formwork systems and related products for construction and technical use.

2.Procedure

  1. 2.1

    The client provides a specification: materials, quantities and the project programme.

  2. 2.2

    Meridion reviews the specification, raises any queries and puts forward suitable alternatives where relevant.

  3. 2.3

    Commercial terms are agreed for the project.

  4. 2.4

    Deliveries may be scheduled in stages in line with the construction programme.

3.Company

The Company serves contractors, developers and infrastructure operators. Sourcing is spread across a number of suppliers, which allows the Company to cover a wide range of categories and to match supply to the scale of each project.
