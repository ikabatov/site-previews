Documentation

Movanta prepares transport documents for each leg of the journey and the commercial paperwork that travels with the cargo, and supports clients with customs declarations and the procedures that come with them.

Five stages of a shipment

  1. Request: cargo details, origin, destination and timing.

  2. Routing: Movanta compares the options and proposes a route and modes.

  3. Agreement: scope and conditions are confirmed.

  4. Transit: carriers are coordinated and the client is updated at each handover.

  5. Arrival: documents are closed out and the cargo is delivered.

Have these ready

Cargo type; approximate weight and volume; origin and destination; target dates; any documents already prepared.
